The ingredient

One plant. One ingredient.

Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera) is a shrub of the nightshade family, native to South Asia and long associated with traditional Ayurvedic practice. Fuel Organics Ashwagandha keeps things simple: Ashwagandha is the ingredient we are launching with.

Botanical name
Withania somnifera
Plant family
Solanaceae, the nightshades
Native range
India, the Middle East and parts of Africa
Also known as
Winter cherry

Fuel Organics makes no medical claims. Ashwagandha supplements are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.
